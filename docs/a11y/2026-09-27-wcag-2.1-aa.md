## Failures to fix

### 1. Primary buttons inside `.prose` have no contrast

- Criterion: **1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum)**, AA, serious
- Pages: `/how-it-works` (“Get started”), `/404` (“Back to Anchor”)
- Viewports: 1280 and 390, consent open and dismissed
- Measured: foreground `#443dff` (`rgb(68, 61, 255)`) on background `#443dff`. Ratio **1:1**. The label is invisible at rest.
- Hover does recover: color becomes `#fbfbfe` on `#2f27ce`. Keyboard and touch users still see the resting state.

Cause: `.prose a` in `apps/web/src/styles/tokens.css` (line 398) is more specific than `.btn-primary` (line 324), so the link color replaces the button label color. The same leak sets `.btn-secondary` inside `.prose` to `#443dff` on `#dddbff`, which measures about **4.66:1** and still passes 4.5:1. Restore both button colors together.

Where:

- `apps/web/src/styles/tokens.css` (`.btn-primary`, `.prose a`)
- `apps/web/src/pages/how-it-works.astro` (the “Get started” link)
- `apps/web/src/pages/404.astro` (the “Back to Anchor” link)

Direction: let `.btn-primary` keep `color: hsl(var(--background))` inside `.prose`. That pair is about **6.06:1**. Axe files this 1:1 case under `incomplete` (`equalRatio`), so the current gate stays green.

### 2. High contrast fails on every public page

- Criterion: **1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum)**, AA, serious
- State: `html.a11y-contrast` (Accessibility → High contrast), consent accepted, 1280×720
- axe violations: **174 nodes**, all `color-contrast`, on all 10 public routes

| Nodes | Ratio | Colors | What |
| ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| 156 | 3.25:1 | `#443dff` on `#050316` | Links, nav, text links. Needs 4.5:1. Example: `.slate-nav a`, `.auth-nav-link` |
| 7 | 3.25:1 | `#050316` on `#443dff` | Primary buttons, including “Send the photo” on `/app` and the homepage slate primary button |
| 10 | 1.21:1 | `#f3f2fe` on `#dddbff` | `/app` only. Chips such as “Kept in her words”, “He agrees himself”, “It comes back” |
| 1 | 1.29:1 | `#fbfbfe` on `#dddbff` | `/family` “Sign in” (`.btn-secondary`) |

Cause: `html.a11y-contrast` in `apps/web/src/styles/tokens.css` (line 508) darkens the page but keeps `--primary` at the brand blue and keeps `--accent` light. Links are forced to `hsl(var(--primary))` (line 530). Primary buttons use the dark background color as their label (line 537). Secondary buttons use light `--text` on the still-light accent.

Direction: choose high-contrast pairs that clear 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text and UI boundaries. Remap `--accent` as well, or stop using it as a button fill in this theme.

### 3. Accessibility panel controls fail in high contrast at 150% text

- Criterion: **1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum)**, AA
- State: high contrast, text scale 150%, panel open, homepage
- 16 `color-contrast` nodes. The new ones, beyond the nav links in finding 2:
  - “Default” and “Larger” (`aria-pressed="false"`): **1.29:1**, `#fbfbfe` on `#dddbff`
  - “Largest” (`aria-pressed="true"`): **3.25:1**, `#050316` on `#443dff`

Where: `apps/web/src/components/AccessibilityWidget.tsx` and `AccessibilityWidget.css`, on top of the token issue in finding 2.

### 4. On a phone, the accessibility button covers the cookie heading

- Criteria: **1.4.10 Reflow** (AA) and, where the heading is covered, unreadable text
- Viewport: 390×844, consent banner open
- `#consent-title` (“Cookies & privacy”) runs from x=20 to x=370. `.a11y-widget-toggle` covers x=239 to x=378 and y=708 to y=752, so the right side of the heading is under the button. `elementFromPoint` on that corner hits the button, not the heading.

Cause: `body.consent-open .a11y-widget` in `apps/web/src/components/AccessibilityWidget.css` (line 14) only lifts the widget by `5.75rem`. On a narrow screen the banner wraps taller than that.

At 1280×720 the same lift clears the banner. axe still reported `#consent-title` and `#consent-body` as “partially obscured” on desktop `/app`; a hit test at 1280×720 landed on the heading and the paragraph, so that desktop incomplete result is not a confirmed defect.

Direction: place the widget above the banner’s real height while `body.consent-open` is set.

### 5. Demo, after “Use a sample line”

Path on `/app` at 1280×900, default theme: “Send the photo” → “Yes, I’ll take part” → “A little later — bring it back” → “Use a sample line”.

- **1.4.3**, AA. `.voice-tag` (“Voice note”): **3.94:1**, `#443dff` on `#cbc8ff`, 12.5px bold. Needs 4.5:1. One node.
- **2.1.1 Keyboard**, A (axe also tags 2.1.3). The private `.chat-log` (`role="log"`) scrolls (`overflow: auto`, scroll height 978px, client height 416px) and has no `tabindex` and no focusable content inside it, so a keyboard user cannot scroll it. The group log did not overflow on this path.

Where: `apps/web/src/components/MvpDemo.tsx` (the private `chat-log` and `.voice-tag`).

Direction: darken or enlarge the voice tag until it clears 4.5:1. Make the private log keyboard-scrollable (`tabindex="0"` when it overflows, with an accessible name).

## What passed

- Official gate: 0 violations, 10 routes × 2 consent states, default theme, first paint, preview build.
- Same routes at 390×844, default theme, consent dismissed: 0 violations. The prose primary buttons are still the 1:1 incomplete result from finding 1.
- Reflow check at 320×640 with consent accepted: `scrollWidth` equals `clientWidth` on all 10 public routes (no horizontal scroll).
- Homepage with the accessibility panel open, default theme, consent dismissed: 0 violations and 0 incomplete.
- Homepage slate primary buttons sit outside `.prose`, so they are not hit by finding 1. They do fail once high contrast is on (finding 2).

## Not in this run

- A screen reader pass.
- A full keyboard walk of every control, beyond the scrollable log in finding 5.
- Signed-in `/family` and `/my-data` actions. Those need a Telegram login. The logged-out first paint was audited.
- axe `incomplete` rows whose only message is that a color could not be determined because of overlap, except where a hit test confirmed the overlap (finding 4).

## Suggested order

1. Fix `.prose a` versus `.btn-primary` (finding 1). Small, and the labels are invisible today.
2. Retune `html.a11y-contrast` tokens, then recheck the panel scale buttons (findings 2 and 3).
3. Lift the accessibility widget clear of the cookie banner on narrow screens (finding 4).
4. Fix `.voice-tag` and the private chat log on `/app` (finding 5).
5. Decide whether `/designs/ink` and `/designs/linen` are still public. If they are, fix finding 6.

After each fix, rerun the gate **and** the state that failed. A green `a11y:web` run does not prove findings 1 through 5 are gone.
