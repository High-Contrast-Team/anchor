## Fixes still open

### 1. Prose styles paint primary buttons the same color as their fill

WCAG 1.4.3. Axe marks this **incomplete**, so the gate stays green. The measured ratio is 1.00:1.

| Page | Control | Colors |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/how-it-works` | “Get started” (`.btn.btn-primary` inside `.prose`) | `#443dff` on `#443dff` |
| `/404` | “Back to Anchor” (same classes) | `#443dff` on `#443dff` |

`.prose a` in `apps/web/src/styles/tokens.css` sets `color: hsl(var(--primary))`, which overrides the button label color. Seen with the consent banner open, dismissed, at 320px, and at text scale 150%.

### 2. High contrast fails 1.4.3 on every public route

Turning on Accessibility → High contrast adds `a11y-contrast` on `<html>`. `--background` and `--text` swap. `--primary` stays `242 100% 62%` (`#443dff`). That purple is too close to both the new dark background and the light label color.

| Pair | Ratio | Need | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `#443dff` on `#050316` | 3.25:1 | 4.5:1 | Nav, footer, auth link, prose and slate links, current-page link. All 10 routes. |
| `#050316` on `#443dff` | 3.25:1 | 4.5:1 | `.btn-primary` and `.slate-btn-primary` (homepage “Start Remembering Together”, “Get started”, “Back to Anchor”, “Sign in with email”). |
| `#f3f2fe` on `#dddbff` | 1.21:1 | 4.5:1 | `/app` step chips (“Kept in her words” and the rest), `.panel-heading`, empty chat copy. Demo colors are not remapped. |
| `#fbfbfe` on `#dddbff` | 1.29:1 | 4.5:1 | `/family` “Sign in” (`.btn-secondary`). |

With text scale 150% and the panel open, the same pairs remain, plus:

| Control | Ratio | Colors |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “Default” and “Larger” (not pressed) | 1.29:1 | `#fbfbfe` on `#dddbff` |
| “Largest” (pressed) | 3.25:1 | `#050316` on `#443dff` |

Text scale 150% on the default theme does not add violations.

## Incomplete, not a measured failure

Axe ratio `0` means it could not compute contrast (overlap, or a background it would not trust). Do not treat these as confirmed fails:

- Consent banner on `/app` while the banner is open (`#consent-title`, `#consent-body`, the Privacy and Cookies links). Other routes did not flag the same banner.
- After the sample line: Nikos’s name and “Yes. I’ll take part.”, and the done-state checkmarks in `.mvp-rail-mark`.
- `/designs/quiet`, listed above.

## Suggested order

1. Stop `.prose a` from restyling `.btn` / `.btn-primary`. That is the only default-theme contrast bug on a public page.
2. Retune `html.a11y-contrast` so links and primary buttons clear 4.5:1, then map the demo chip and panel colors that stay on `#dddbff`.
3. Darken `.voice-tag` and make the private chat log keyboard-scrollable.
4. Darken `.ink-action-index` on the ink study, then recheck quiet by eye.
