## Verdict

The repository gate passes:

```
axe WCAG 2.1 AA: 10 routes × 2 consent states — 0 violations
```

That run is the default theme at the Playwright viewport (1280×720). It loads each public route twice: cookie banner open, then dismissed. It does not open the Accessibility panel, turn on high contrast or larger text, or walk the demo past the first screen. It also ignores axe `incomplete` results, which is how the 1:1 buttons below stay green.

The failures to fix later are in sections 1 to 4.

## 1. Primary buttons inside `.prose` fail 1.4.3

**Impact:** serious. **Theme:** default. **axe:** `incomplete` (the gate does not fail).

| Page | Control | Colors | Ratio | Required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/how-it-works` | “Get started” (`a.btn.btn-primary`) | `#443dff` on `#443dff` | 1.00:1 | 4.5:1 |
| `/404` | “Back to Anchor” (`a.btn.btn-primary`) | `#443dff` on `#443dff` | 1.00:1 | 4.5:1 |

Text is 15px, weight 600. The same 1:1 result is present at 320×640. axe’s message is “Element has a 1:1 contrast ratio with the background”.

**Cause.** Both links sit in `<article class="prose">`. In `apps/web/src/styles/tokens.css`, `.prose a` sets `color: hsl(var(--primary))` and `font-weight: 600`. That beats `.btn-primary`, which sets `color: hsl(var(--background))` and weight 700. The label is the same purple as the fill.

**Passes next to it.** The secondary button in the same row is `#443dff` on `#dddfff`, 4.66:1, which clears 4.5:1.

**Later fix.** Set color and weight on `.prose a.btn` so prose links cannot restyle buttons. Primary labels should stay `hsl(var(--background))`.

## 2. High contrast theme fails 1.4.3 on every public page

**Impact:** serious. **axe:** `violation`. **Gate:** not included.

The Accessibility control stores `highContrast: true` in `anchor-a11y-prefs-v1`. `BaseLayout.astro` adds `html.a11y-contrast` before paint. Tokens live in `apps/web/src/styles/tokens.css`. The theme keeps `--primary` at `242 100% 62%` (`#443dff`), sets `--background` to dark ink (`#050316`), and sets `--text` to light paper. `--accent` / `--soft-bg` stay the light lavender (`#dddbff`).

Confirmed with consent dismissed, 1280×720, on all 10 public routes. Homepage at default text size: 21 failing nodes, all **3.25:1**.

| Pair | Examples | Foreground | Background | Ratio |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Links (nav, footer, in-page) | “How it works”, “Get started”, “My data” | `#443dff` | `#050316` | 3.25:1 |
| Primary buttons | “Start Remembering Together” | `#050316` | `#443dff` | 3.25:1 |

A second pass with text scale 150% and the Accessibility panel open still fails, and adds pairs that use the unchanged light accent:

| Pair | Where | Foreground | Background | Ratio |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Demo progress chips | `/app` “Kept in her words”, “He agrees himself”, “It comes back” (`.mvp-rail li`, `color: var(--ink-muted)` on `background: var(--soft-bg)`) | `#f3f2fe` | `#dddbff` | 1.21:1 |
| Secondary button | `/family` “Sign in” | `#fbfbfe` | `#dddbff` | 1.29:1 |
| Text-size buttons, inactive | Accessibility panel (`.a11y-widget-scale button`) | `#fbfbfe` | `#dddbff` | 1.29:1 |
| Text-size button, pressed | Accessibility panel (`background: var(--brand)`; `color: hsl(var(--background))`) | `#050316` | `#443dff` | 3.25:1 |

Node counts on that second pass: `/` 16, `/app` 26, `/family` 18, `/how-it-works` 18. Required ratio stays 4.5:1 for these sizes. One family step label was large enough that axe asked for 3:1, and 1.29:1 still failed.

**Later fix.** In `html.a11y-contrast`, give links, primary buttons, secondary buttons, chips, and widget controls their own foreground and background pairs that each clear 4.5:1. Do not leave `--primary` as the link color on the dark page, do not use the dark `--background` token as button label text, and do not leave `--soft-bg` as a light chip on a dark page with light text.

## 3. Demo, after “Use a sample line”

**Impact:** serious. **Theme:** default. **Gate:** misses this state (first paint of `/app` is clean).

Path on `/app`, consent already accepted: “Send the photo”, then “Yes, I’ll take part”, then “A little later — bring it back”, then “Use a sample line”.

| Rule | WCAG | Node | Detail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `color-contrast` | 1.4.3 | `<span class="voice-tag">Voice note</span>` | 3.94:1, `#443dff` on `#cbc8ff`, 12.48px bold. Need 4.5:1. |
| `scrollable-region-focusable` | 2.1.1 | Private `.chat-log` (`role="log"`) | Scroll height 978px, client height 374px, `tabIndex` -1, overflow `auto`. Keyboard users cannot move the overflowing log. |

The group `.chat-log` on that path does not overflow (302px in a 302px box) and was not flagged.

**Cause.** `.voice-tag` in `apps/web/src/components/MvpDemo.css` uses `color: var(--brand)` on a translucent brand wash (`rgba(68, 61, 255, 0.12)`), which blends to about `#cbc8ff`. The private log is a scroll box with no tabindex and no focusable child that axe can use.

**Later fix.** Give `.voice-tag` a solid fill and a label color at or above 4.5:1. Make the private log keyboard-scrollable (`tabindex="0"` only when it actually overflows is enough).

## Not a confirmed failure

With the banner open, axe marked `#consent-title` and `#consent-body` on `/app` as `incomplete` (“partially obscured”). The banner is `position: fixed` at `z-index: 50`. Hit-testing the title and body landed on the banner text. The demo composer extends underneath it until consent is dismissed. Do not treat this as a 1.4.3 failure unless a later pass shows the banner text itself is covered.

High contrast step numbers (`span.slate-step-num`, `aria-hidden="true"`) are axe `incomplete` because the text is one character. Not a failure.

## Out of scope

- A signed-in family record with real memories
- A manual screen reader pass
- The Telegram login window
- Criteria axe cannot decide on its own, including 1.4.12 text spacing

## Suggested order

1. Stop `.prose a` from recoloring `.btn-primary` (section 1).
2. Retune `html.a11y-contrast` so links, buttons, chips, and widget controls each clear 4.5:1 (section 2).
3. Fix `.voice-tag` and the private `.chat-log` after the sample-line path (section 3).
4. If `/designs/ink` and `/designs/linen` stay reachable, fix those two contrast pairs (section 4).
5. Extend `apps/web/scripts/a11y-check.mjs` so the gate also runs `html.a11y-contrast`, walks `/app` through “Use a sample line”, and fails when a measured 1:1 contrast is left in `incomplete`.
