Anchor · Demo script

The 6-minute presentation as one story: the problem with the website on the screen, a day in Sofia's family with Anchor, and the close back on the website. Four people speak. Two family members post, the family clock moves to the next morning, and Sofia answers her private memory of the week with one tap.

Before you start

  • Eleni's phone shows the family group throughout. Sofia's phone shows her private chat with Anchor.
  • The Operator shows the phones side by side on the screen, and starts the stopwatch at the Narrator's first word.
  • Eleni has the staged photo of a child's first day at school ready, with the caption "Maria's first day of school! She wore her new red backpack."
  • Alexandros has the staged beach photo ready, with the caption "Sunday lunch at the beach with the whole family!". He posts one photo only.
  • Sofia has "Family moments" and "Talk to me by voice" on, and "Call me on the phone" off. Eleni has "Family moments" and share offers off.
  • The website https://anchor-open26.vercel.app is open in a browser tab on the Operator's laptop. The screen shows its landing page when the presentation starts.
  • The technical checks are done (see the technical runbook).

3 · The close

3:00Closer

"Sofia saw her family's week without searching the chat. She answered with one tap, and Eleni called her. Nobody acted for her.

Anchor says it is not a person. It shares nothing without a yes, and it keeps a painful memory without ever bringing it back on its own. And when a message pretends to be Eleni and asks for money, Sofia forwards it to Anchor, and Anchor tells her to call Eleni first.

Anchor runs live today, in the group chat the family already uses. And you can try it yourself, on our website: anchor-open26.vercel.app.

On "our website", the Operator switches the screen from the phones to the website.

Anchor keeps the family's story, so nobody drops out of it."

The audience sees: Both screens stay on the group, then the landing page of the website. The screen stays on the website for the Q&A.

Questions to expect

"Isn't a bot in the family chat annoying?" Anchor answers a message that names Anchor, with "Anchor," or with "@anchor_family_bot". A message without "Anchor," gets an answer in two cases only: a general request, such as "Give me a memory", or a request for memories or photos of someone or something already in the family record, when the model agrees that it is a request. When Anchor is unsure, it says nothing.

The numbers: in a replay on the live family record, 10 of 12 memory requests got the album, and 0 of 14 ordinary messages got an answer, among them "Can you send me the photos from yesterday?". A reply between two people never reaches Anchor.

The easy no: every offer has a no, an unanswered offer fades, and "stop" turns Anchor off for one person.

"Can Anchor make things up?" Anchor answers only from the family record and the group chat, names who shared the moment, quotes their words, and then shows the photo it came from. Code checks every number, name, and quote in the answer against that moment; an answer that fails becomes "Here is what the family record says 💛" and the photo. In a replay, 10 of 10 answerable questions got a checked answer, 9 of 10 questions the record does not answer got "The family record doesn't say", and the check stopped the tenth.

"Is Sofia safe from scams?" Sofia forwards a suspicious message to Anchor. When it uses a family name to ask for money or a code, and it did not come from that person's Telegram account, Anchor tells her to call the person first, and offers to tell them. Anchor never says a message is certainly safe. In a test, 6 of 6 scam runs got the warning, and 0 of 6 ordinary requests did.

"What about accessibility?" Sofia never needs to read or type. A spoken request, such as "What did I miss?", gets the answer of the typed request. The voice notes and the call say what each photo shows, without names or judgments, for a member who cannot see the photo well.

If a beat fails

No ❤ in beat 1 after 10 seconds: Eleni continues with the jump. /fastforward saves every open post before the clock moves, so the photo still joins the memory.

The group sees "/fastforward 1" or "⏩ It's now …": the command went out as a normal message, not from the command menu. Continue.

No memory on Sofia's phone after 15 seconds: Sofia sends "Send me a moment" to Anchor in private. A memory of one moment arrives with the same buttons, and the story continues.

The call question names Alexandros instead of Eleni: Sofia taps "Yes, ask Alexandros", and Alexandros says Eleni's line.

The website does not load in the close: the Operator keeps the phones on the screen, and the Closer says the line with the address.

Two beats fail: the Operator stops the live demo and plays the screen recording. The speakers say their lines over the recording.

Rehearse twice with a stopwatch. The run must end before 6:00. It ends at about 3:50, which the first rehearsal must confirm.
